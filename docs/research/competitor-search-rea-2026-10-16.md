## Per product

- **Teal.** The page says "Find your dream role with over 3 million jobs" and a free job tracker with a Chrome extension for 40+ boards (tealhq.com/tools/job-tracker, fetched 2026-10-16). `/tools/job-board` returned 404. The old capture `/var/tmp/rea-comp/teal/teal_home.txt` is a Cloudflare 403 block; Apify got through. Whether the 3M is real or sourced from ATS: not verifiable here. A search snippet (third party, not Teal) says ATS-direct and recommendation-only inside the app; one third party puts it at 4,600 listings. Treat the 3M as unproven. No MCP seen.
- **Sonara.** Alive on 2026-10-16: sonara.ai serves "Recent Jobs" cards with Apply buttons and "We continuously scan millions of openings" (title "Sonara: AI Job Search Tool & AI Auto Apply"). Third parties disagree: loopcv.pro says discontinued; resumly.ai says BOLD bought it and relaunched. The fetched page is the evidence it is live. Sources not named.
- **Rezi.** `rezi.ai/tools/job-search`: "This is not a job board. We've got over 1.3M jobs sourced directly from company websites ... Updated hourly ... all leading applicant tracking systems." The MCP page (`rezi.ai/resume-mcp`) is a resume tool: `claude mcp add rezi --transport http https://api.rezi.ai/mcp`, OAuth sign-in, and the example "Find PM jobs in Austin and tailor them" (agent "picks the strongest match"). So the MCP does reach Job Search, but the tool names are not published: NOT FOUND. Ranking method: NOT FOUND.
- **6figr JobGPT.** Blog (fetched) says 34 tools at `https://mcp.6figr.com/mcp`, key from 6figr.com/account starting `mcp_`, local fallback `npx -y jobgpt-mcp-server`. It names categories only: Job Search, Profile and Salary, Job Hunts (saved searches with auto-apply), Applications, Resume (including "calculate match scores"), Outreach. Individual tool names seen only in the marketing demo: `search_jobs`, `generate_resume_for_job`, `apply_to_job` (`/var/tmp/rea-comp/6figr/auto-apply.txt`). Job source: NOT FOUND. It auto-applies, which Job360 must not.
- **Kickresume.** AI Job Matcher FAQ: "It reads your resume and scores live job openings ... ranks every opening by how closely it fits ... strongest first"; "compare your profile against thousands of live openings". Job Board "Search thousands of jobs". Source of postings: NOT FOUND. Matching is Kickresume's own model (page mentions GPT-4.1 for the resume builder, not matching).
- **LazyApply.** Home: "automatically apply to all the jobs on platforms like Greenhouse, Dice, Indeed and Ziprecruiter". It also has `lazyapply.com/jobs`: "Search 100,000+ work from home jobs", "Found 172,133 jobs", with H1B and tech-stack filters and a "Most Relevant" sort. So it searches as well as autofills. The listing source is not named. Caps are 15/150/1500 applications a day.
- **Jobright.** Home capture: "Total Jobs 8,000,000+, Today's New Jobs 400,000+", "Personalized AI Job Matches ... no fake listings and early alerts". Sourcing is not on its own site. A WebSearch summary of its FAQ and a third-party review say boards, company sites and ATS; treat as unverified. `jobright.ai/faq` is a 404.
- **Simplify.** Help article: "Every day, Simplify surfaces a fresh set of roles ranked against your profile, preferences, and experience", inputs are experience, skills, roles, locations, work authorization, resumes and thumbs up/down feedback; each match has a "Why This Job is a Match" section. Home page: "Fresh roles, added hourly", "Trusted by 2M+". Where the roles come from and the score formula are not published: NOT FOUND.
- **Careerflow.** The "71-tool MCP server" is not supported by anything I could open. `careerflow.ai/mcp` returns 404, the home and pricing captures have no MCP text, the WebMCP probe found 0 tools (`/var/tmp/rea-comp/careerflow/webmcp.json`), and a search found nothing. Verdict: not found; do not repeat the claim.
- **freehire.me.** Home counters: 4,737,131 postings, 436,530 companies, 264 sources, 114 ATS platforms. `/sources` (counted "3 hr. ago"): 298 sources, 2,197,552 jobs indexed. The two numbers differ; the 4.8M figure is "postings tracked". `/how-it-works`: ingest straight from the employer's ATS or careers page, several times a day, per-source schedules; derive tags from a closed dictionary; LLM enrichment from a queue; dedup by role and company, preferring the employer's own ATS over an aggregator copy; atomic reindex. `/agents`: MCP is `freehire-mcp` via npx; search needs no key; "An agent never submits an application"; rate limits 600/min reads, 300/min agent search; "market fit" scores a skill set against demand. Free and open source.
- **jobspipe.dev.** Home: "10.9 million active job postings from 30+ sources, as of October 14, 2026" (you quoted 11.3M; the page says 10.9M). `/agent`: hosted MCP `claude mcp add --transport http jobspipe https://mcp.jobspipe.dev/mcp`, two functions (job search, tech-stack scan), sources include Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Personio, Recruitee, Teamtailor, Pinpoint, Breezy, Paylocity, Manatal, LinkedIn public postings, Indeed, Y Combinator. It states what it does not collect (iCIMS, JazzHR, Paycom, Taleo, Jobvite, Rippling, Dayforce, SuccessFactors, Zoho Recruit). Freshness: 24 h free, 6 h mid, 1 h top tier. It collects "postings that anyone can read without logging in". Tool names beyond "job search / stack scan": NOT FOUND.
- **trackly.** `usetrackly.app`: "SCANNING 5,566 COMPANIES", "270,881+ roles", "swarm of AI agents monitors company career pages ... pulling new roles directly from the source", FAQ "5,500+ technology-focused companies across 49+ ATS types". The repo README (`/var/tmp/refs/trackly-cli/README.md`, commit 31b8d75) says 250K+ jobs, 5,400+ companies, 40+ ATS types, 58 local + 55 hosted MCP tools, hosted at `https://mcp.usetrackly.app/api/mcp`. `docs/trackly-tools.md:85`: `sort` is `newest` or `match` (match needs a resume). The site claims it "replicated the ATS scoring algorithm" for the match probability; no method is published. The company list is curated and server side; not in the repo.

## ATS lists we could add

Verified by counting `backend/src/hunt_kit/companies.json`: 3,677 rows = ashby 1,875 + lever 1,166 + greenhouse 631 + workable 5. By origin: jobsync 3,560, career-ops 117. There are no rows for the other board types the kit can already fetch (`BOARD_SOURCES` in `hunt_kit.py:62`: smartrecruiters, recruitee, personio, teamtailor, bamboohr, workday; fetchers in `kit_ats.py:164-421` for the last five; smartrecruiters is a listed source).

- **Demand evidence (freehire `/sources`, jobs read per vendor):** Workday 411,625; SmartRecruiters 118,900; Greenhouse 92,079; Ashby 43,047; Paylocity 45,330; Manatal 48,498; Workable 39,663; Lever 28,684; BambooHR 26,842; JazzHR 25,793; Teamtailor 24,337; Recruitee 17,378; Breezy 12,871; Personio 11,526; Rippling 9,930; Join 9,237; Pinpoint 7,544. Vendors with an adapter in our kit but zero rows in `companies.json`: Workday, SmartRecruiters, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Personio. Workable has 5 rows against 39,663 jobs read at freehire, so it is the most under-filled.
- **Where company lists come from (no vendor publishes one):** jobspipe's own docs say for Pinpoint, Breezy, Teamtailor and Personio "there is no list of customers". The only open list I opened is **Feashliaa/job-board-aggregator** (pinned `ab0f66f` in `hunt_kit.py:1536`): README says 20,000+ companies across seven ATS (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, BambooHR, iCIMS, Paylocity, Workday), and about 95,000 company identifiers across 20+ ATS domain patterns, built by scanning Common Crawl CDX indexes for ATS URLs and extracting slugs by regex. The kit already pulls five of those lists at run time (`UNIVERSE_ATS`, `hunt_kit.py:1537`), and the default skips bamboohr and workday as "~24k boards". That method is reproducible for Workable, Recruitee, Personio, Teamtailor and SmartRecruiters.
- **Licence flag (needs an owner check):** that repo's README says its `data/` company lists are CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial; commercial use needs permission). `backend/src/hunt_kit/NOTICE.md` describes the project as MIT and says it lists `job-board-aggregator` rows, but `companies.json` has no such rows today (the lists are only fetched at run time). Check this before shipping those lists in a commercial product. The code is MIT; the data is not.
- **Hosted indexes are not list sources.** freehire (open source, per its own site) and jobspipe expose postings through an API, not company lists. trackly's 5,566-company list is private. Their per-ATS job counts are the only reusable fact, and they are only a guide to which vendors to fill first.

## Mission note

Every competitor above that finds or ranks jobs does it with its own AI, or auto-applies. The ones closest to Job360's rule 4 are freehire and jobspipe: public indexes the user's own agent queries over MCP, with no ranking by the vendor. That is evidence for adding a store-style source tool at most, not a search or ranking pipeline.
