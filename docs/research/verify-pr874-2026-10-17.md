## 4. Problems, by size

1. **New sources starve in a default run.** In a 5-min GB+remote run, Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever (about 4.3K boards) used the whole budget. freehire, the feeds, Workday and the national services ran **0**.
   - Fix: run the cheap, high-yield sources (connectors' REST, feeds, freehire, national services) first, and the big board sweeps last. Or give each group its own share of the time.
2. **No SuccessFactors boards** in `companies.json`, so that fetcher never runs by default.
3. **Dead starter tenants:** all 8 Taleo, and most Eightfold ("Not authorized for PCSX"). Prune them, or re-harvest.
4. **A dead Workday site is retried once per title** (`kit_ats.py:183-189`). Stop the board after its first non-200.
5. **Workday is slow:** 20 boards took 172 s.
6. **One freehire error ends that query's paging** (`hunt_kit.py:1505`). JobTech had one transient failure that looks the same as "blocked".
7. **The default title excludes hid Job Bank's matching rows.**
8. **Smaller:**
   - The Workday rotation jumps when the cached list changes.
   - The `hunt.md` LinkedIn limits (3 pages, 5 s apart) live only in the README.
